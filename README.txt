MEGA MAN COMPLETE WORKS 1-6
ENGLISH TRANSLATION COLLECTION — FINAL FEATURE SUMMARY

This README describes the major changes from the original Japanese PlayStation
Rockman Complete Works releases to the six translated builds in this collection.
It lists the resulting features once, without development versions, intermediate
edits, or the history of visual and wording refinements.

SHARED TRANSLATION AND PRESENTATION

- English localization of the PlayStation menus and supporting text: Mode Select,
  pause/options screens, controls, save/load and password prompts, and bonus-mode
  interface text where present.
- English Navi guidance, enemy/boss Database material, Dr. Light's Lab text,
  equipment descriptions, and related help and unlock messages where present.
- English text in graphical assets as well as ordinary text strings, including
  title/menu branding, labels, and relevant gallery or completion graphics.
- Mega Man naming replaces Rockman branding in the localized titles and text.
  Recurring characters, weapons and support items use consistent English names.
- Weapon Select labels use a consistent readable English treatment. Long names
  are abbreviated to fit their original label/lifebar areas, such as Q-BOOMERANG.
  Native menus retain their original layouts and compact presentation.
- English story, ending and credit material as applicable to each game.
- Original and Navi modes remain available, along with each title's existing
  Complete Works features. The translation preserves the original art direction,
  stage layouts and title-specific numerals/backings.

MEGA MAN 1

- English title, Mode Select, loading and PlayStation interface graphics, including
  the Mega Man logo in the green Navi pause panel.
- English Navi hints, Database/Lab material, weapon names and equipment help.
  Labels cover the Mega Buster, six Robot Master weapons and Magnet Beam.
- R2 shortcut to toggle between Magnet Beam and the Mega Buster after Magnet Beam
  has been acquired, without refilling its energy.
- Restored the complete 34-sample gameplay sound-effects bank from the original
  NTSC sound program, including landing, firing, weapons, damage, pickups,
  platforms, doors, death and one-up effects. The Complete Works music and its
  separate interface sound bank remain intact.
- English Boss Attack interface and associated menu text.

MEGA MAN 2

- English title and PlayStation branding, retaining the original striped numeral
  2 and red title backing.
- English opening/interface text, Navi guidance, Database/Lab material, weapon
  and equipment text, and password/button prompts.
- English Weapon Select labels for all eight Robot Master weapons, the Mega
  Buster, Items 1-3 and Energy Tanks.
- R2 quick switching for the acquired support items.
- English Boss Attack interface and associated menu text.

MEGA MAN 3

- English title and Mode Select branding, retaining the original numeral and
  title-backing geometry.
- English PlayStation interface, Navi guidance, Database/Lab material, weapon
  names and support-item text, including Rush Coil, Rush Marine and Rush Jet.
- R2 quick switching for the acquired Rush support forms.
- English epilogue and ending Robot Master biography cards, retaining the
  information and numbering from the Japanese presentation.
- English Boss Attack interface and associated menu text.

MEGA MAN 4

- English title artwork and subtitle: A NEW AMBITION!!
- English opening story and Cossack/Kalinka/Wily dialogue, plus the story-ending
  presentation and credits.
- English PlayStation interface, Navi hints, Database/Lab material, weapon-award
  text and equipment descriptions.
- Localized weapon/support menus, including Mega Buster naming, the eight Robot
  Master weapons, Rush equipment, Wire and Balloon.
- English Mission and Sound Room interface text, plus completion/staff graphics.

MEGA MAN 5

- English title artwork and subtitle: PROTO MAN'S TRAP.
- English opening story, castle dialogue and story-ending material, including
  Dr. Light's rescue, Proto Man's intervention and the escape sequence.
- English PlayStation interface, Navi hints, Database/Lab material, weapon-award
  text, equipment help and credits.
- Localized weapon/support menus, including Mega Buster naming, the eight Robot
  Master weapons, Rush equipment, Super Arrow, Beat and the Mystery Tank.
- Proto Man's boss-health emblem uses P in place of the Japanese Blues B.
  Mega Man retains his own health-bar icon.
- English Mission and Sound Room interface text. Mission help and pause displays
  accommodate the English equipment descriptions.

MEGA MAN 6

- English title, opening graphics and Mega Man branding.
- English opening story, Mr. X reveal, Wily capture ending and newspaper pages.
- English PlayStation menus, Navi hints, Database/Lab entries, equipment help,
  Mission text, Sound Room text, gallery and completion material.
- Localized weapon and support labels, including Mega Buster naming and English
  Normal, Jet and Power form badges.
- Slide-jump behavior: jumping can cancel a slide.
- Corrected charged-shot sound and the full-charge weapon-get blast behavior.
- Password-screen control changes and quit/cancel freeze mitigation.
- English bonus/completion presentation with memory handling that permits the
  translated bonus resources to load.

VERIFICATION SCOPE

This is a feature summary, not a claim of exhaustive hardware certification.
Recorded project checks include emulator-rendered Original/Navi menus, populated
inventories, bonus menus and the story endings for 4-6. Some sequence, inventory
and unlock checks used RAM assists. Patch reproduction and disc-sector integrity
checks are recorded in the per-game verification data. Full uninterrupted
playthroughs and physical PlayStation/PocketStation operation are not certified.
